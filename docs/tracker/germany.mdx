# Germany

<StatusBadge status="Public Pilot" />

Announced project with a public sandbox, branded D-You from September 2026. The state-driven version is scheduled to launch on 2 January 2027, then to open the market to private wallet providers in 2028. Germany has allocated 79.3 million euros to the project through 2026; the initial launch supports identification and credential presentation, with qualified signatures, pseudonymous logins and payments to follow. The source code has been published and a bug bounty programme is opening, with a PIN reset service due back in October 2026. Critics question whether the full wallet can be ready on time as fewer than half of member states are on track for the end-2026 deadline.
