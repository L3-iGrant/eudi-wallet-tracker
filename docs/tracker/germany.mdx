# Germany

<StatusBadge status="Public Pilot" />

Announced project with public sandbox. State-driven version scheduled to launch on 2 January 2027, then opening the market to private wallet providers in 2028. Germany has allocated 79.3 million euros to the project through 2026; the initial launch supports identification and credential presentation, with qualified signatures, pseudonymous logins and payments to follow. Critics question whether the full wallet can be ready on time as fewer than half of member states are on track for the end-2026 deadline.
