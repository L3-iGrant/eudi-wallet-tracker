# Germany

<StatusBadge status="Public Pilot" />

Announced project with a public sandbox. On 9 September 2026 the BMDS unveiled the name and logo of the state wallet, d-you, with launch scheduled for 2 January 2027 and around 40 launch partners from business, science and administration, including Bundesdruckerei, KfW, Deutsche Post and Vodafone. Applications ready on launch day are to let users prove their age, conclude legally binding contracts and open a bank account; the market then opens to private wallet providers in 2028. Germany has allocated 79.3 million euros to the project through 2026; the initial launch supports identification and credential presentation, with qualified signatures, pseudonymous logins and payments to follow. The source code has been published and a bug bounty programme is opening, with a PIN reset service due back in October 2026. Critics question whether the full wallet can be ready on time as fewer than half of member states are on track for the end-2026 deadline.
