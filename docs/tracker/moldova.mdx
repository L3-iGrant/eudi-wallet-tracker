# Moldova

<StatusBadge status="Closed Pilot / LSP" />

Closed pilot with the EVO Wallet, run by the eGovernance Agency and developed in line with the EUDI Wallet framework. A public EVO Wallet Sandbox offers conformance tests for relying parties, checking that a verifier accepts valid credentials and rejects manipulated ones, plus credential offer and credential claims tools, without registration. Full integration still requires an agreement with the agency and a certificate from STISC through the national trust scheme, which opened to public and private organisations on 1 April 2026. In September 2026 four banks, Moldindconbank, FinComBank, EuroCreditBank and Victoriabank, began accepting EVO digital credentials for customer identification: the customer scans a QR code and chooses which of the requested data to share, removing the need to present a physical ID document. Thirty relying parties are at various stages of connection, with at least 20 expected to be operational by the end of 2026.
