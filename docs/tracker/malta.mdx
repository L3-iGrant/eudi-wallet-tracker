# Malta

<StatusBadge status="Closed Pilot / LSP" />

The Malta Digital Innovation Authority (MDIA) has issued a call for tenders for implementing Malta's EUDI Wallet, covering secure identification, age verification, storage of driving licences and electronic signing. Identity Week and Namirial note Malta is expected to deliver only partial availability by deadline.
