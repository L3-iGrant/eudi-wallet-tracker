# Finland

<StatusBadge status="Closed Pilot / LSP" />

DVV leads the national wallet, branded Suomi.fi Wallet, with a public developer repository linked to its work but no confirmed government sandbox. On 2 July 2026 DVV selected Scytáles Ab, from nine tenders, to implement the wallet's core technical components, the backend systems and the mobile application. The wallet is planned for release in 2027, alongside a relying party register, and is to offer strong online identification, electronic signatures and reliable sharing of personal data with services in Finland and across the EU.
